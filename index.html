About me

I love to build and to find out how things work. As a child playing with Lego, to a young adult restoring a car, tinkering with technology has always been a part of my personality.

I am a technically minded individual with a wealth of experience engineering and sales. Originally graduating in Automotive Engineering, I pursued a more commercially related path which helped me develop my professional communication and administrative skills.

I am now looking to do the next step in my career by working as a Software Developer. I hold a master’s in computer science and have gained professional experience by working within a university software development company. I wish to utilise my experience in sales, together with engineering knowledge to provide a well-rounded service and insights.
